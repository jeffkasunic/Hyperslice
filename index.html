THE GEOMETRY

What are you seeing?

The solid is the part of the tesseract that lies at the chosen W coordinate. Here X, Y, Z, and W all measure position. In spacetime, the fourth coordinate measures time.

The tesseract is one of the regular shapes followed from a point up to higher dimensions in Platonic Solids in Higher Dimensions.

THE GEOMETRY

What are you seeing?

The solid is the part of the tesseract that lies at the chosen W coordinate. Here X, Y, Z, and W all measure position. In spacetime, the fourth coordinate measures time. See how slicing works, one dimension down.

The tesseract is one of the regular shapes followed from a point up to higher dimensions in Platonic Solids in Higher Dimensions.

02 / ONE DIMENSION DOWN

How a slice works

We can't see four dimensions, but we can see the same idea one dimension down. Push a 3D shape through a flat wall and the wall only ever holds one 2D slice of it. Our 3D space does the same to a 4D shape.
